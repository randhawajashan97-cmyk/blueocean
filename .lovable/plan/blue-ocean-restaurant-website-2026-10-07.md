# Blue Ocean restaurant website

## Goal
Build the complete mobile-friendly Blue Ocean website from the supplied brief, with a prominent WhatsApp ordering experience using the listed number, **+91 98775 09435**.

## Website experience
- Replace the blank home screen with a polished single-page restaurant site using ocean-blue accents, warm neutral surfaces, clear typography, and food imagery labeled as representative rather than actual Blue Ocean dishes.
- Add a compact header and easy navigation to Menu, About, Reviews, Hours, and Visit.
- Create an inviting first section with **View Menu**, **Call Now**, **Get Directions**, and **Order on WhatsApp** actions.
- Present only the verified business details: address, phone, 4.9 rating from 35 reviews, estimated ₹200–₹400 spend, and dine-in, kerbside pickup, and no-contact delivery.
- Add menu category placeholders for pizzas, fries, and sandwiches without inventing item names or prices, plus a call link for today’s menu.
- Add review highlights, visit/contact information, a Google Maps directions link, and clearly marked provisional hours of 7:00 am–10:00 pm pending weekly confirmation.

## WhatsApp ordering
- Add a fixed WhatsApp button that stays easy to reach on mobile and desktop.
- Open an accessible slide-over order panel instead of sending visitors away immediately.
- Collect customer name, phone, order details, pickup or delivery choice, delivery address when needed, and optional notes.
- Validate the required fields, conditionally show the delivery address, and offer clear close/reset states.
- Generate a neatly formatted, pre-filled WhatsApp message and open it to **+91 98775 09435** for the customer to review and send.
- Clarify that sending the message is an order request and the restaurant should confirm availability, price, and timing.

## Quality and presentation
- Use reusable controls, accessible labels, keyboard-friendly panel behavior, visible focus states, and responsive layouts.
- Add subtle motion while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Add Blue Ocean–specific search and social metadata for the restaurant and location.
- Verify the key flow at desktop and mobile sizes: open the panel, enter an order, switch fulfillment methods, and reach the correctly prepared WhatsApp link.

## Assumptions
- WhatsApp uses the listed business phone number because no separate WhatsApp number was provided.
- The order panel is a WhatsApp message builder; it will not process payments or claim that an order is confirmed.
- Menu items, exact prices, and the weekly hours remain clearly marked for owner confirmation.
